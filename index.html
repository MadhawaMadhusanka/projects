Madhawa Madhusanka

Maharagama, Sri Lanka • Maker & STEM Educator

I am a technology enthusiast, maker, and STEM educator who loves bringing ideas to life through hardware, 3D design, and code. Alongside building my own systems, I am passionate about teaching robotics and guiding students to build their own Arduino and IoT projects.

What I do & build:

  • Robotics & Embedded Systems: Developing automated solutions, custom robots, and electronics using ESP32, Arduino, and various sensor modules.
  • STEM Teaching & Project Mentoring: Teaching robotics to young learners and helping students design, troubleshoot, and complete their school or competition Arduino and IoT projects.
  • 3D CAD & Prototyping: Designing mechanical parts, enclosures, and custom mechanisms in Onshape and bringing them into the real world through 3D printing.
  • Software Development: Building full-stack web systems (PHP, MySQL, Tailwind CSS) and cross-platform mobile apps (Flutter, Firebase).

I enjoy tackling hands-on engineering challenges and sharing my knowledge with the next generation of makers. Always open to connecting, mentoring, and collaborating on innovative hardware or software projects!
